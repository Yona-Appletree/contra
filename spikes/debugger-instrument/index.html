The debugger as a move-review instrument

One screen, three states of it. Pixels are always 2× — a view gets bigger by holding more floor, never by growing its pixels. The wall of cards is the instrument: every card is the same beat, and clicking one opens that one alone with the 3d beside it. ⟲ loop is also the switch — watching the dance, the cards are the hall's minor sets; looping a move, the same cards become that move's rigs.

“2x pixels is good … I want to see multiple views at 2x. if possible click one of them and get details about exactly that one … the 3d view is important … timeline and graphs in one tabbed view, the timeline basically is a graph … keep the separate tape.”

1  On a dance — a card per minor set, plus the couples waiting out

Six minor sets and two waiting couples, all on the same beat, so the eye catches the one set whose hey is late or whose end dancer has nobody. The wide card across the top is the whole hall at the same 2×. The text is beside them, lit at the running call and editable.

butter.dance — 6 minor sets · 112 bpm

2  After ⟲ loop — the same wall, now the rigs of the one move

The looped call in nine contexts it will meet: the middle of a long set, both ends, the other side, entered facing the other way, at six beats and at ten, out of a chain, and with nobody where the neighbour should be. Same beat in all nine, so they compare.

butter.dance — looping neighbor swing · 9 rigs

3  One card opened — and the tabbed panel folded away

The same hall, still 2×, given more floor rather than fatter pixels; the 3d view of exactly that rig beside it, with yaw and pitch; what the call bound in that context; and only its own complaints. One click on ▾ folded the timeline panel to its tab row and the detail took the height.

butter.dance — rig the top end · nobody above

4  What the mock is not

Every move begins and ends in becket lines, so any one of them loops alone; the hey is a spline through four waypoints, not a hey; the complaints are written by hand and inject a defect puts one on the wall so a bad card can be seen carrying it. What is real: the text is butter.dance, the moves and their beats are parsed out of it live, the 3d is drawn from the same bodies the pixels are, and editing a beats = n or deleting a call re-times every picture on this page on the keystroke.
