1. The transport, in the strip under the hall

The five glyphs on the wall under the canvas, centred, the 32 px mute top-right of the canvas. Ruled: 1F with outlines. The three samples are the outline weights the strip switches between; the phone shows the chosen one. The readout under the strip says what the last press did.

2. The notecard

Ruled: 2F. White stock, faint rules, the phrase letter in the margin, one call to a line with its beat count faint before the ⓘ. Tap a call to go to its beginning; tap ⓘ to open the move (a popover here, a bottom sheet on the phone). Shown for all three dances so the "‖" line and the two-beat call are in view.

3. The tune, in its own box

Ruled: 3B. Paper like the card, under it; the dropdown pins a tune for this dance. A stave is a phrase — eight bars, sixteen beats — with its letter at the left and the calls written under the bars they take. Click any bar: the evening goes to the start of the move that bar is in. Top-right, the potatoes: four pixel spuds, dim until the band counts the dance in, then lit one a beat. Press → the potatoes in the strip (or |◀◀) and play to watch them.

4. Put together

The ruled set: 1F outlined glyphs in the strip, the 2F notecard, the 3B tune box with the potatoes, the 32 px mute. Phone on the left (ⓘ opens a bottom sheet), laptop on the right (ⓘ opens a popover). The dance select, tempo and trails keep their row under the strip.
