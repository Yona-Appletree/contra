Hands Four · two dancers

Follow-up spike to spikes/hall (vision: "The hall, rendered"; D13, D14, D15, D21; Q4 and Q5 are now ruled). It builds on the gate-2 rulings rather than re-asking them: true overhead only, people about 1.5× the hall's (shoulders 11 px wide, arm reach 15 px, roughly 4 cm per pixel), anti-aliasing allowed for sprites and motion, positions still at 1/256 px, integer zoom for display. Rendering contract: the body is drawn procedurally from above (feet, skirt, shoulders, head with a face sliver and a nose) and rotated per facing, the head turns separately to look at the partner. Joined hands always stack the same way: the robin's hand on top, fingers down, the lark's underneath, palm up, so the robin's arms draw over the lark's. Arms are puppets: each hand is a floor point with a height that both dancers compute from the same figure; the arm is two 7.5 px bones with fixed length in three dimensions, solved by two-bone IK with the elbow pole pointing down and out and then projected onto the floor, so a planar arm can never exceed 15 px and a hand point out of reach makes the arm point at it and fall short, so hands only meet when the dancers are close enough. Spacing: 14 px chest to chest in a two-hand hold (about 55 cm), lines 18 px further apart; dancers walk in before taking hands, and every take and release is animated rather than snapped. Movement is deliberately quiet: contra glides, so the beat shows as small foot and shoulder motion, not bounce. Everything is canned keyframes for one 64-beat sequence on the tune's clock, not an engine.

0. The pair

Rulings at gate 3 (2026-09-13): passes, move on to the plan. Arms as fixed-length 3D bones are the answer to the arm problem; joined hands stack robin on top, lark underneath, always; contra movement is smooth, only a little motion with the beat. Tweaks applied after the gate: the swing's outstretched hands sit closer to the pair, and the allemande turns each body about 20° toward the partner rather than a straight line, since arms cannot take pressure coplanar with the torso. Later: the balance's forward rock still crowds the heads; extending the bones model to head and shoulders for shading is open.
