feat(contra): star's default hold is a wrist grip, not a centre pile

The user's own hall puts the giving hand on the wrist of the dancer
ahead round the star, a forearm's length back from the centre along
their arm, so four hands sit at four distinct points on a small ring
rather than one shared pile -- each arm stays a little bent. A new
`hold: "wrist" | "hands-across"` parameter defaults to "wrist";
"hands-across" is today's shape, unchanged, gated behind the
parameter. The wrist hold's giving hand is not a shared HandJoin (only
the giver has a hand there), so figureChecks.ts gets a local
`wristJoined` assertion rather than a new choreo/testing/trajectory.ts
helper. describe rewritten in the user's words, marker removed.
